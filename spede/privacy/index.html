SPEDE — Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026

SPEDE (“the app”) is made by Matti Manninen, an independent developer working under the Gyrosaur brand in Oulu, Finland. In short: SPEDE does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no app-operated network service.

Microphone

SPEDE asks for microphone access only so you can record your own samples. Audio is recorded only while you hold the REC control. The resulting sample is stored on your device. Recordings are never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Audio files and kits

Recorded audio, audio you import, edited samples, Local Kits, staged changes and settings are stored locally on your iPhone or iPad, inside the app. They are included in your own device backups (iCloud or computer) according to your device settings.

When you choose a Roland SPD-SX storage location in the iOS Files picker, iOS gives SPEDE access to that location only. SPEDE remembers that choice with a local security-scoped bookmark so it can reopen the same device later. SPEDE writes to the SPD-SX only when you explicitly save, and keeps local safety backups of the kit files it changes. You can revoke access by disconnecting the device or removing the app.

Sharing that you start

You can export a WAV file or share SPEDE’s diagnostic log through the standard iOS share or document interface. You choose the destination or recipient. Nothing is shared automatically. The diagnostic log stays on your device until you share it.

What is not done

  • No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No data is collected as defined for App Store privacy disclosure, and no data is used to track you across apps or websites.
  • No data is sold, shared or transferred to the developer or any third party.

Retention and deletion

Local Kits, samples and settings remain on your device until you delete kits or samples in the app or delete the app. Files you save to an SPD-SX or export elsewhere remain at that destination until you remove them there. Because the developer receives no data, there is nothing held by the developer to delete.

Children

SPEDE does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

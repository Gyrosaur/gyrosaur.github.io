SPEDE — Support

SPEDE is a sample and kit workspace for iPhone and iPad. It works on its own with Local Kits, and it can transfer kits and samples to the original Roland SPD-SX.

Contact: gyroaudio@gmail.com
Matti Manninen · Gyrosaur · Oulu, Finland. Replies usually within a few days.

Getting started without hardware

  1. On the first screen, tap USE LOCAL KIT WITHOUT HARDWARE. You may need to scroll down to see it.
  2. Choose MAIN or SUB above the pads, then tap LOAD to import audio or hold REC to record with the microphone.
  3. Tap the pad to play it. Open the sample editor to trim, fade, adjust the output level, shape the EQ, rename or export a WAV.
  4. Press SAVE KIT to keep your changes. Create more Local Kits in Kit Manager.

Connecting an SPD-SX

  1. Put the original SPD-SX in WAVE MGR mode and connect it to your iPhone or iPad with USB.
  2. Press the 3×3 device control at the top left of SPEDE. In the Files picker, open Browse → Locations and choose the top-level SPD-SX drive. It may appear as Untitled.
  3. Choose a kit, make your changes and press SAVE KIT. Keep the cable connected until SPEDE confirms the save, then disconnect USB and restart the SPD-SX.

SPEDE supports the original SPD-SX storage format. SPD-SX PRO compatibility is not currently claimed.

Common questions

REC does nothing. Allow microphone access for SPEDE in Settings, and disconnect any Bluetooth headset that is using the call-audio route.

SPD-SX is not found. Check that the unit is in WAVE MGR mode, then reopen the connection guide from Help and choose the top-level SPD-SX or Untitled location.

A save to the SPD-SX failed. SPEDE keeps your staged changes and a local backup. Do not repeat the save before reading the message, and do not disconnect USB while SPEDE is writing.

Reporting a problem

  1. In SPEDE, open Help (the ? button) → SUPPORT → SHARE DIAGNOSTIC LOG.
  2. Email the log to gyroaudio@gmail.com with your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, SPEDE version, whether an SPD-SX was connected, and the step that failed.

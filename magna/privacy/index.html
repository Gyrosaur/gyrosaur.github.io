Magna — Privacy Policy

Effective 24 September 2026

Gyrosaur Magna (“Magna”, “the app”) is made by Gyrosaur, an independent developer in Oulu, Finland. In short: Magna does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. Everything the app senses is processed on your iPhone and stays there.

What the app uses and why

  • Camera and LiDAR — to scan your room, recognise it again later and place the synth panels, piano rolls and other objects in it. Camera images are processed live and are not recorded or saved.
  • Face tracking (TrueDepth camera) — optional. When you turn on face control, facial expressions (for example how open your mouth is, eye direction, eyebrows) are turned into sound parameters and drawn as fading lines on screen. Face data is used only in the moment, on the device; it is never stored, never sent anywhere and never used to identify you.
  • Microphone — optional, off by default. When you turn on “Mic”, the app listens for the sound of blowing to add breath noise to the synthesizer. Audio is analysed live and is not recorded or saved.
  • Motion sensors — in Air mode, the phone’s movement controls the sound. Motion data is used live only.
  • MIDI — if you connect MIDI devices or apps, the app receives and sends musical notes and controller values. Nothing else is sent.
  • Local network — used only by a developer diagnostics feature that is off in the App Store / TestFlight version.

What is stored

Your scanned rooms (a 3D map of the room’s surfaces), the objects you place in them, your piano roll patterns and your settings are saved only on your iPhone, inside the app. They are included in your own device backups (iCloud or computer) according to your device settings. Magna has no accounts and no servers.

What is not done

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.
  • No data is used to identify you or to build a profile of you.

Deleting your data

Delete a room in the app’s room list (swipe left), or delete the app to remove everything it has stored.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
